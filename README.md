# Multicom Bond Issuance Case Study

A team case study on how Multicom could raise approximately RUB 1 billion in gross proceeds for four years. We compared two non-callable bond issues with a callable bond that could be refinanced after year two.

## Pricing and funding cost

1. Estimated a benchmark yield to maturity of **12.00%** from a five-year comparable bond with a RUB 1,000 face value, a RUB 100 annual coupon, and a market price of RUB 927.90.
2. Discounted each four-year bond's scheduled payments at its required yield to estimate the issue price. The callable bond used an assumed 120 basis point premium, bringing its required yield to 13.20%.
3. Calculated the issuer's annual funding cost as the internal rate of return (IRR) on net proceeds, annual coupons, and principal repayment. The callable scenarios include the cost of issuing new debt in year two.

## Results

| Structure | Price per bond | Funding cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| I. Non-callable bond, 12% coupon | RUB 1,000.00 | 12.51% |
| II. Non-callable bond, 10% coupon | RUB 939.25 | 12.53% |
| III. Callable bond, 12% coupon, refinanced after year two | RUB 964.45 | 12.21% probability-weighted scenario IRR |
| III. Callable bond held to maturity | RUB 964.45 | 13.73% |

| Year-two refinancing rate | Assumed probability | Funding cost for option III |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10% | 20% | 13.24% |
| 8% | 50% | 12.31% |
| 6% | 30% | 11.37% |

**Case recommendation.** Option III had the lowest weighted cost under the stated assumptions. Option I fixed the four-year cost at 12.51% and was cheaper in the 10% refinancing scenario. A follow-up calculation using the case cash flows puts the break-even refinancing rate against option I at approximately **8.42%**, assuming the replacement bond is issued at par with the same principal and the stated fees.

## Scope

The case sizes each issue to a gross proceeds target. After initial fees, the three structures yield approximately RUB 984 million to RUB 985 million in net proceeds. A RUB 1 billion net funding target would require resizing the issues. The yield premium and scenario probabilities are case assumptions. Prices and cash flows are shown to two decimal places.
